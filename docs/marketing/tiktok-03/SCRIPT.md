# TikTok carousel 03 · "bin day"

He pretends to forget their two-year anniversary all day. Every hint she drops, he answers
with the bins. At 23:47 she says goodnight, hurt. He sends a countdown. It runs out at
midnight, the exact start of their anniversary, and it ends in fireworks.

The fake-out is the oldest format on this app because it never stops working. People watch
the whole thing to see if he really forgot, and the relief at the end is what makes them
send it on.

Her: Maya. Him: never named.

## The gift

answer is a sale.

it counting down in real time. Set it for a few minutes after you start recording.
