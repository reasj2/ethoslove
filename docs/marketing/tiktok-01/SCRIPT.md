# TikTok carousel 01 · "he made me apologise"

Eleven slides. Six are text screenshots, already rendered in `short/`. Five are your own
screenshots of the gift, and they slot in between slide 5 and slide 6.

The v4 cut got three comments because the story was only cheating, and cheating is
background noise on this app. Nobody argues about it. This version is not about the girl in
the photo. It is about him taking her apart until she is the one apologising, and then
buying his way out of it at 2am. That is what people cannot scroll past without typing.

Her: Mia. Him: Jay. Her best friend: Lea. Mia types long, lowercase, emotional. Jay types
one word, late, no punctuation, and never once answers the question. Keep that contrast, it
is the whole joke.

## The three that carry it

**Slide 2 is the lie collapsing in real time.** It isn't him, then it is him, then she has a
name, then the name is wrong, then it is a middle name, then she is in his mum's bed. People
count the lies out loud in the comments. That is why they are stacked in one frame.

**Slide 5 is the trap.** Her real apology at 11:48pm, a grey heart on it, "Read 11:49 PM",
no reply. Then three hours later, "u up" and a gift. Nothing happens on that slide, which is
exactly why people stop on it. Write nothing over it.

**Slide 11 is the ending.** She brags to her best friend that they are okay, and the receipt
lands in the same breath. Ending on "2 weeks ago" hands the argument to the comments instead
of finishing it yourself.

Grey bubbles on slide 11 are Lea, not Jay. If someone asks in the comments, that is another
argument you did not have to write.

## The gift, which you shoot yourself

Build it at tryethos.io with the values below. Open it on your phone, Share → Add to Home
Screen, then open it from the home screen so there are no Safari bars. Shoot the five frames
listed as 6 to 10 above. There is a built copy at **tryethos.io/g/jayandmya** if you want to
see what it should look like first.

Everything in it is enormous and none of it is specific. That is what love bombing sounds
like: volume instead of detail. He never mentions the photo, the girl, the gym, or the two
days of silence. He turns the dial to maximum and waits.

## Caption, tags, sound

Caption: `he made me apologise for going through his phone and then made me this 😭 am i
crazy or`

The question at the end is the whole strategy. "Am I crazy" is an instruction to comment,
and everyone answering it has to say his name.

Tags: `#storytime #gaslighting #relationshiptok #redflags #textstory #imessage #fyp`

Sound: slow, sad, recognisable, played straight. Never a funny sound. The carousel has to
look sincere for the comments to be angry. Check what is trending the day you post and drop
it to about 40% so the text stays readable.

Post between 7pm and 10pm. Slideshows get pushed hardest in the evening.

## Making the comments work for you

The carousel is the bait. The comment section is the product.

- **Pin your own comment first:** `guys he did answer eventually. he said "why are u still
  on this"`. A fake update keeps people arguing under the pin.
- **Reply to "girl leave him" with** `i did. friday.` It closes the story, it reads like a
  real person, and it doubles the replies on the top comment.
- **Reply to "what website is that"** with only `ethos, its called jar of reasons`. No link,
  no pitch. TikTok throttles comments with links, and anyone who wants it will search.
- **Never defend him.** Every reply should make people angrier at him and sadder for her.
- If someone says the S is a typo, reply `it isnt`.
