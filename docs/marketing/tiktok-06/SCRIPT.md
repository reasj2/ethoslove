# TikTok carousel 06 · "we need to talk"

**Lever: dread, then relief.** "We need to talk" is the most feared text anyone gets. He sends
it at 16:03 and makes her wait until 9. She's sure it's a breakup. It's the first "i love you".

At 18 to 25, the first "i love you" is a bigger moment than any present, and it costs nothing.
Everyone watching either remembers theirs or is waiting for it. That is why this lands, and why
it was wrong to end it on a flat nobody that age could offer.

Her: Ava. Him: Theo. Three chat slides, five gift frames.
