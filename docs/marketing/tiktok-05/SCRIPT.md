Her boyfriend sends "hbd" at 9am and nothing else all day. She tells him she feels
invisible, and he reads it and doesn't reply.

At 20:28 he tells her to put headphones in and turn the big light off. He's built her a
cinema: her name on the marquee, candles that go out when she actually blows at the phone,
confetti. Then he tells her to look out the window, and he's standing outside holding a cake.

"Everyone forgot my birthday" is a feeling nearly everyone has had, which is why this
format spreads past couples. People send it to friends too.
